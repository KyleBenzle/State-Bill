Chairman Hillyer, Vice Chair Grendell, Ranking Member Galonski and members of the House Civil Justice
Committee, thank you for the opportunity to provide proponent testimony on HB508.



My name is Mark Ludwig and I am the Founder of Americans for Equal Shared Parenting. After my story
was featured on TV and went viral, I began to be contacted by literally thousands of non-custodial
parents in similar situations. My FB Live videos have literally been viewed in the tens of millions of
times which tells me that this is an issue that resonates with people across the country.

The current ‘winner take all’ system creates an adversarial relationship between parents who feel
compelled to win at all costs instead of learning how to collaborate as equal partners in raising children.

This system creates confusion in children who do not understand why one parent is treated as an
unequal/inferior parent and the children feel like a visitor to that parent.

There is currently a wave of equal shared parenting legislation occurring across the country. Arkansas,
Kentucky, Texas, Missouri, Tennessee and Oklahoma have all passed recent legislation which is leaning
this direction and over 25 other states have had similar legislation introduced.



Mr. Chairman and members of the committee, thank you again for allowing me to provide testimony on
HB 508. I would be happy to answer any questions.
