                      HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
                    FAMILIES AND AGING COMMITTEE

                                       Minutes for
                                    May 14, 2024
Chair Schmidt called the meeting of the House Families and Aging Committee to order at
11:04 AM. The roll was called. A quorum was present.

The Chair brought the minutes from the April 23, 2024 meeting forward for approval.
The minutes were approved without objection.

The Chair brought up HB 512 for its first hearing.

The Chair called upon Vice Chair Miller who motioned to accept substitute bill
L_135_0414-3. Without objection the substitute bill was accepted.

Representative Pavliga was present and gave sponsor testimony to the committee and
answered questions asked by committee members.

The Chair concluded the first hearing on HB 512.

The Chair brought up HR 459 for its first hearing.

Representative M. Miller and McClain were present and gave sponsor testimony to the
committee and answered questions asked by committee members.

The Chair concluded the first hearing on HR 459.

The Chair brought up HB 14 for its fifth hearing.

Anthony Slosser was present and gave proponent testimony to the committee. The
witness answered questions asked by committee members.

Mark Durbin was present and gave proponent testimony to the committee. The witness
answered questions asked by committee members.
Donald Hubin was present and gave proponent testimony to the committee. The witness
answered questions asked by committee members.

Bridget Mahoney was present and gave opponent testimony to the committee. The
witness answered questions asked by committee members.

Mary O’Doherty was present and gave opponent testimony to the committee. The witness
answered questions asked by committee members.

Amy Roehrenbeck was present and gave interested party to the committee. The witness

no questions by committee members.

The Chair concluded the fifth hearing of HB 14.

The Chair adjourned at 12:59 P.M.
