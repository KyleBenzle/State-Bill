If an obligor or obligee files a request for a court hearing on a revised amount of child support to be paid under a court child support order in accordance with section
3119.63
of the Revised Code and the rules adopted under section
3119.76
of the Revised Code, the court shall conduct a hearing in accordance with section
3119.66
of the Revised Code.
