                                                                        H.B.ANo.A3181




1                                        AN ACT

2    relating to the enforcement of a court order for possession of or

3    access to a child and related order modifications.

4             BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:

5             SECTIONA1.AASubchapter        B,   Chapter    156,    Family    Code,   is

6    amended by adding Section 156.107 to read as follows:

7             Sec.A156.107.AAMODIFICATION OF ORDER ON FINDING OF CONTEMPT

8    FOR DENIAL OF POSSESSION AND ACCESS.            A finding by the court that a

9    conservator is in contempt of court for the denial of court-ordered

10   possession of or access to a child and has previously been found in

11   contempt of court at least three times for failure to comply with

12   the terms of an order providing for possession of or access to the

13   child     constitutes    a     material       and    substantial        change   of

14   circumstances sufficient to justify modification of an existing

15   court order or portion of a decree that provides for the appointment

16   of   a   conservator    or   that   sets      the   terms   and   conditions     of

17   conservatorship or for the possession of or access to the child.

18            SECTIONA2.AASection 157.165, Family Code, is amended to read

19   as follows:

20            Sec.A157.165.AAPROBATION OF CONTEMPT ORDER.              (a)    Except as

21   provided     by   Subsection    (b),    the    [The]   court      may   place    the

22   respondent on community supervision and suspend commitment if the

23   court finds that the respondent is in contempt of court for failure

24   or refusal to obey an order rendered as provided in this title.

1          (b)AAThe court may not place the respondent on community

2    supervision and suspend commitment if the court finds that the

3    respondent:

4                  (1)AAis in contempt of court for the failure or refusal

5    to obey an order for possession of or access to a child; and

6                  (2)AAhas previously been found in contempt of court at

7    least three times for the failure or refusal to obey an order for

8    possession of or access to a child.

9          SECTIONA3.AASection          157.167,      Family   Code,    is   amended      by

10   amending   Subsection      (c)    and   adding    Subsection      (e)   to    read   as

11   follows:

12         (c)AAExcept as provided by Subsections [Subsection] (d) and

13   (e), for good cause shown, the court may waive the requirement that

14   the respondent pay attorney ’s fees and costs if the court states the

15   reasons supporting that finding.

16         (e)AAThe     court    may    not   waive     the    requirement        that    the

17   respondent pay attorney ’s fees and costs if the court finds that the

18   respondent has previously been found in contempt of court at least

19   three times for the denial of court-ordered possession of or access

20   to the child who is the subject of the proceeding.

21         SECTIONA4.AASection          157.168,      Family   Code,    is   amended      by

22   amending Subsections (a) and (a-2) and adding Subsection (d) to

23   read as follows:

24         (a)AAUnless a party shows good cause why the order should not

25   be rendered [Except as provided in Subsection (a-1)], a court shall

26   [may] order additional periods of possession of or access to a child

27   to compensate for the denial of court-ordered possession or access.

1            (a-2)AAThe additional periods of possession or access:

2                   (1)AAexcept as provided by Subsection (d), must be of

3    the same type and duration of the possession or access that was

4    denied;

5                   (2)AAmay       include          weekend,       holiday,        and    summer

6    possession or access; and

7                   (3)AAmust occur on or before the second anniversary of

8    the date the court finds that court-ordered possession or access

9    has been denied.

10           (d)AAIf the court finds that the person denying possession or

11   access has previously been found in contempt of court at least three

12   times for the denial of court-ordered possession or access with

13   respect to the child who is the subject of the proceeding, the

14   additional periods of possession of or access to the child ordered

15   by    the   court   under    this    section       must     be,   in   total,     twice   the

16   duration of the periods of possession and access that were denied.

17           SECTIONA5.AASections 157.168(a-1) and (c), Family Code, are

18   repealed.

19           SECTIONA6.AA(a)        Section 156.107, Family Code, as added by

20   this Act, applies to a suit for modification that is pending in a

21   trial court on the effective date of this Act or that is filed on or

22   after that date.

23           (b)AAThe changes in law made by this Act to Sections 157.165

24   and    157.168,      Family    Code,      apply        to    a    suit      affecting     the

25   parent-child relationship that is pending in a trial court on the

26   effective     date   of     this    Act   or    that   is    filed     on    or   after   the

27   effective date of this Act.

1         (c)AAThe change in law made by this Act to Section 157.167,

2   Family Code, applies only to an enforcement order rendered on or

3   after the effective date of this Act.   An enforcement order rendered

4   before the effective date of this Act is governed by the law in
