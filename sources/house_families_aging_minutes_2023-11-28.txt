                      HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
                    FAMILIES AND AGING COMMITTEE

                                       Minutes for
                                 November 28, 2023
Chair Schmidt called the meeting of the House Families and Aging Committee to order at
11:19 AM. The roll was called. A quorum was present.

In the absence of Representative Denson, the Chair designated Representative Liston as
Committee Secretary for today’s meeting.

The Chair brought the minutes from the June 20, 2023 meeting forward for approval. The
minutes were approved without objection.

The Chair brought up HB 173 for its second hearing.

There was no testimony. The Chair recognized Representative Robb Blasdel for a motion
to favorably report HB 173 back.

The clerk called the roll. The motion passed with 9 affirmative votes and 0 negative
votes.

The Chair concluded the second hearing of HB 173.

The Chair brought up HB 14 for its fourth hearing.

The Chair recognized Representative Robb Blasdel for a motion to accept Sub. Bill
L_135_1898. Without objection, the motion was accepted.

The Chair concluded the fourth hearing of HB 14.

The Chair adjourned at 11:24 A.M.
