 “Friendly parent” factors – consideration

 No provision.                                        Requires a court to consider whether a parent has intentionally done
                                                      any of the following, after determining that a presumption of equal
                                                      decision-making rights and responsibilities or equal parenting time
                                                      under a parental rights and responsibilities order has been rebutted:
                                                             Misled the court to cause an unnecessary delay, increase the
                                                              cost of litigation, or persuade the court to give that parent a
                                                              preference regarding the presumption that has been
                                                              rebutted;
                                                             Made false allegations against the other parent of harm to
                                                              the child;
                                                             Communicated false information to law enforcement, a
                                                              public children services agency, or the court in order to gain a
                                                              tactical advantage in a proceeding to determine the
                                                              allocation of parental rights and responsibilities.
                                                      (R.C. 3109.0423.)

                                                      (R.C. 3109.0424(A)(2)(b) and 3109.0425(A)(2)(b)).
