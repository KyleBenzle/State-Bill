An administrative support order issued pursuant to former section
3111.21
of the Revised Code prior to January 1, 1998, that is in effect on  the effective date of this section shall remain in effect on and after  the effective date of this section and shall be considered an administrative support order issued pursuant to section
3111.81
of the Revised Code for all purposes.
