Legislation 2017 Regular Session House Bills House Bill 492 House Bill 492 Actions | Amendments Last Action signed by Governor (Acts, ch. 163) Title AN ACT relating to temporary custody orders. Bill Documents Current/Final Introduced Bill Request Number 1635 Sponsors J. Petrie, R. Mills, D. Osborne Summary of Original Version Amend KRS 403.280 to create a presumption of joint custody in temporary custody orders and to have prior parental custody agreements become the court's orders.
