                                     Committee Minutes

COMMITTEE:             Civil Justice
CHAIRPERSON:           Brett Hudson Hillyer
DATE:                  May 10, 2022
TIME:                  12:30 PM
ROOM:                  Room 114

Chair Hillyer called Civil Justice Committee to order at 12:33 PM.

The Clerk called the roll. With a quorum being present, the minutes from the 05.03.2022 meeting
were accepted.

Chair Hillyer appointed Rep. Leland the temporary committee secretary.

Chair Hillyer called forward HB 545 for its fourth hearing. Chair Hillyer moved to amend HB
545 with amendment AM_134_3196. Chair Hillyer explained the amendment. Chair Hillyer
asked if there were any objections to the amendment. Without any objections, AM_134_3196
passed and became part of HB 545.
Chair Hillyer moved to amend HB 545 with AM_134_3182. Chair Hillyer explained the
amendment. Chair Hillyer asked if there were any objections to the amendment. Without any
objections, AM_134_3182 passed and became part of HB 545.
Chair Hillyer called forward Lou Tobin to provide Interested Party Testimony. Lou Tobin
provided Interested Party Testimony. Chair Hillyer asked some questions. Lou Tobin answered
those questions.
The following written testimony was submitted:
Rick Skilliter
Vice Chair Grendell moved to favorably report HB 545 out of House Civil Justice Committee.
Representative Seitz seconded the motion. The clerk called the roll. HB 545 passed with a vote
of 14-0 and was favorably reported out of House Civil Justice Committee.

Chair Hillyer called forward SB 224 for its third hearing. Chair Hillyer moved to amend SB 224
with amendment AM_134_3139. Chair Hillyer explained the amendment. Chair Hillyer asked if
there were any objections to the amendment. Without any objections, AM_134_3139 passed and
became part of the bill. Chair Hillyer asked if there were any witnesses present to testify. With no

Chair Hillyer called forward HB 508 for its fourth hearing. Chair Hillyer called forward Rep.
Creech to provide testimony regarding the changes in the proposed sub bill. Representative
Creech provided testimony. Vice Chair Grendell, Representatives Stewart, Lampton, and White
all asked questions. Anthony Slosser also came forward to help answer some questions. Chair
Hillyer made some comments. Chair Hillyer moved to adopt L_134_1415-6 as the substitute bill.
Chair Hillyer asked if there were any objections. Without any objections, L_134_1415-6 was
adopted as a sub bill to HB 508.
This concluded the 4th hearing on HB 508.

Chair Hillyer called forward HB 472 for its first hearing. Chair Hillyer called forward Rep.
Ingram to provide sponsor testimony on HB 472. Rep. Ingram provided sponsor testimony. Chair
Hillyer, Reps. Merrin, Jarrells, Lampton, and Cutrona all asked questions. Rep. Ingram answered
those questions. This concluded the first hearing on HB 472.

Chair Hillyer called forward HB 556 for its second hearing. Chair Hillyer called forward Alex
Campbell to provide proponent testimony. Alex Campbell provided proponent testimony. Chair
Hillyer asked Rep. Swearingen a question. Rep. Swearingen answered the question. This
concluded the second hearing on HB 556.

Chair Hillyer called Sub SB 199 for its second hearing. Chair Hillyer called forward Susan
Racey to provide proponent testimony. Susan Racey provided proponent testimony. Rep. Stewart
asked questions. Susan Racey answered the questions.
The following written testimony was submitted:
Paul Pfeifer
Kirk Roberts
This concluded the second hearing on Sub SB 199.

Chair Hillyer gave Rep. Stewart time to talk about his resolution regarding Civil Rule 39(c). Rep.
Stewart provided comments. Vice Chair Grendell asked a question. Rep. Stewart answered the
question.

With no further business before the committee, Chair Hillyer adjourned Civil Justice Committee
at 1:53 PM.




Brett Hudson Hillyer, Chair
