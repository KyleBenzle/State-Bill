## Not adopted

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| Support enforcement remedies triggered by parenting denials | R.C. 3109.05(D); federal IV-D structure |
| Renaming the CSEA throughout the Code | Hundreds of conforming changes; IV-D designation risk |
| A new state FSEA office | Duplicates existing county infrastructure |
| Parental support framed as support of the parent | Drifts toward federal "alimony" (42 U.S.C. 659(i)(3)) |
| Retroactive accounting credits | Bradley Amendment; R.C. 3119.83 |
| A parent-paid FSEA fee | Instruction; the penalty-vs-fee problem |
| Court motion plus good cause for accounting (the Louisiana model) | Creates a judicial decision; the annual cap does the work instead |
