# DOCUMENT 3 — SECTION-BY-SECTION OUTLINE (REVISION 4)

The bill amends 13 sections, enacts 5 (in **bold** below), and repeals none. It also has 4 uncodified
sections.

| Part | Subject | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| One | Parents start equal | 3109.03, 3109.042, 3109.401 |
| Two | The 50/50 starting point; restored status | **3109.044**; 3109.04, 3109.043, 3109.12 |
| Three | Only a court changes an order; no pay to parent | **3109.045**; 3109.053, 3109.055 |
| Four | Allegations must be resolved | **3109.046** |
| Five | The Family Support Enforcement Agency | 3125.10; **3109.047**; 3109.051(K); 2705.031 |
| Six | Parental support and accountable child support | 3119.01; **3119.024** |
| Seven | Criminal interference and referral | 2919.23 |
| Eight | Transition, totals, funding, data | Uncodified 3–6 |

## PART ONE

- **3109.03:** equality for all parents, married or not. No preference by sex or marital status.
- **3109.042:**
  - (A) Both parents are custodians once paternity is established. No lawsuit is needed.
  - (B) Status is not a schedule.
  - (C) Existing orders govern; the parent uses the restoration motion.
  - (D) The rape and sexual-battery bar is sex-neutral.
- **3109.401:**
  - New finding: fit parents ordinarily determine their children's interests; third parties are not
    presumed better judges.
  - The standard-schedule policy is made subject to 3109.044.

## PART TWO

**3109.044:**
- (A) The 50/50 presumption, including at temporary orders. "Substantially equal" means 45 percent
  or more of overnights.
- (B) If not rebutted: a shared parenting decree. No plan is needed.
- (C) The presumption does not apply in three cases:
  - an agreement (approved unless harmful);
  - a **full-hearing** protection order against the parent, while in effect;
  - a 3109.04(C) offender.

  Ex parte orders and consent agreements govern by their terms only.
- (D) **Rebutted only on five grounds:**
  - endangerment;
  - inability or unwillingness;
  - distance or school;
  - repeated denial;
  - a child 14 or older.

  Conflict, objection, self-made non-cooperation, and unfound allegations don't count. The (F)(1)
  factors count only as they bear on the grounds.
- (E) If rebutted:
  - written facts;
  - a maximize-time schedule;
  - a **90-night floor**, with four exceptions and least restrictive means.

  The floor is never a target, and fewer than 90 nights is not itself evidence of a crime.
- (F) Decision-making: split if needed; the status quo on deadlock.
- (G) Safety restrictions on a preponderance. No conviction is needed.

**3109.04:**
- (A) Allocation follows 3109.044.
- (C) Evaluation costs are subject to 3109.053.
- **(E)(1)(c): restoration motion.**
  - No change in circumstances is needed, and no plan.
  - Granted unless there is a violence history, an immediate threat, or a statutory bar.
  - **The schedule, support, and school stay unchanged.**
  - The first schedule motion is decided under 3109.044.
  - After a denial, a 12-month wait to refile.

**3109.043:** a 28-day hearing; no inference from a temporary order.

**3109.12:** the presumption applies.

## PART THREE

**3109.045:**
- (A) Only a court changes an order.
- (B) A GAL's recommendation is evidence only. A party may call and cross-examine the GAL. Delegation
  to a non-judge is void.
- (C) A parenting coordinator handles implementation only, with independent review on objection.
- (D) A safety plan binds only with written consent or a court order.
- (E) Emergency savings.

**3109.053:**
- (A) Classes and counseling as now, without the parent-cost rule.
- (B) **No pay to parent.** No right, time, or status may be conditioned on paying for a
  court-required service. A service may be required only if it is free to the parent.
- (C) Preserved: fees, costs, fee awards, restitution, and remedial orders after a court finding of
  misconduct.

**3109.055:** conciliation costs are subject to 3109.053.

## PART FOUR

**3109.046, the 36-month limit subject to one 45-day extension:**
- (A) Definitions.
- (B) No restriction without an order.
- (C) A 90-day hearing on request.
- (D) The limit:
  - 36 months;
  - a court-set hearing at 30 months;
  - one 45-day extension;
  - the restricted parent's delay is excluded;
  - one period for all allegations about pre-restriction conduct;
  - the prior order then resumes.
- (E) After resolution.
- (F) Good-faith reports protected. Unsubstantiated, uncharged, dismissed, acquitted, or mistaken does
  not itself mean false.
- (G) **Clear and convincing** proof of falsity, knowledge, and purpose brings make-up time, fees, and
  a change in circumstances.
- (H) Scope.

## PART FIVE

**3125.10:** each CSEA is also the county's **Family Support Enforcement Agency**. Its FSEA duties
are non-IV-D and separately funded.

**3109.047:**
- (A) The FSEA administers orders. It does not decide custody, status, safety, or contempt.
- (B) A complaint within 60 days; no fee. A **parenting coordinator first** for 14 days, with
  exceptions.
- (C) Inquiry: 14 days to obtain the order and notify; 14 days to respond.
- (D) A determination within 45 days. For each denial:
  - make-up time of the same type and duration;
  - **$100 / $250 / $500** within 24 months, paid to the wronged parent.
- (E) Unclear orders and safety claims go to the court as contempt.
- (F) Mailing; objection within 14 days; a hearing within 28 days, decided anew; entry if no
  objection. Payments are civil judgments, **not support**.
- (G) One record: ordered vs. received.
- (H) **Automatic referral**, plus optional contempt, after three entered denials in 12 months, 14+
  consecutive days, or concealment or removal from Ohio.

**3109.051(K):**
- (1) Mandatory make-up time.
- (2) A 28-day hearing and a prima facie case.
- (3) Lawful excuses.
- (4) Child refusal.
- (5) Three findings in 24 months are a change in circumstances.

**2705.031(B)(2):** the FSEA may file contempt for the parent.

## PART SIX

**3119.01:** both components are child support for all state and federal purposes.

**3119.024:**
- (A) Two components: **parental support** (household, unaccounted) and **accountable child support**.
- (B) The total is unchanged. Accountable child support is the greater of 30 percent or child care;
  the court may vary this on findings. Medical support is separate.
- (C) Qualifying expenditures, including child savings deposits.
- (D) An annual request through the FSEA. Within 60 days, records showing **where** the money was
  spent (payee, date, amount). A bank, credit card, or payment-app statement is enough; cash payments
  are listed in a signed statement. **No itemizing and no receipts.** Redaction; assigned support
  exempt.
- (E) A shortfall is determined by the FSEA and entered by the court, then credited **prospectively**
  over 12 months.
- (F) **No self-help.** No suspension, and no change to support already due.

## PART SEVEN

**2919.23:**
- (A)–(D) unchanged.
- (E) Clarifications:
  - court-ordered time without an excuse is "without privilege";
  - a reasonable reading of an unclear order is not a crime;
  - it is not a civil matter.
- (F) Referral from the FSEA or a court. Within 60 days plus one 30-day extension, a charge or a
  declination. No reasons; discretion preserved; **a referral does not establish guilt**; a
  declination is unreviewable; no bar to a later prosecution or a 2935.09 complaint.

## PART EIGHT

- **Section 3, transition:**
  - **the FSEA and support components start one year after enactment**;
  - existing support orders may be restated into the two components without changing the total.
- **Section 4:** the total support calculation is unchanged.
- **Section 5:**
  - forms;
  - funding (state appropriations, existing local resources, and 669b "to the extent legally
    available"; no misuse of IV-D funds);
  - data.
- **Section 6:** S.B. 174 conformity.
