# DOCUMENT 8 — CUT LIST

Every provision of the previous draft (Revision 1, 28 September 2026), and what happened to it.

**Key**
- **CUT**: deleted; existing Ohio law already does it, or it isn't needed.
- **MERGED**: its substance survives in a shorter provision elsewhere.
- **KEPT**: retained, usually shortened.
- **REVERSED**: replaced by the opposite policy, as instructed.

Revision 1 amended 4 sections, enacted 19, repealed 1, and ran about 10,100 words of
statutory text. Revision 2 amends 8 sections, enacts 3, repeals none, and runs about 4,600, including
existing text shown for context.

---

## A. Whole-draft structure

| Rev. 1 | Fate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Two-track drafting, [A] S.B. 174 / [B] current law, on every cross-reference | **CUT** | It doubled the length of every section and made the bill unreadable to anyone but LSC. Rev. 2 is drafted against current law only; see Document 2, Part 3. |
| Section 6, contingency switching between tracks | **CUT** | Replaced by a one-sentence statement of intent (Section 6). |
| § 3109.081 short title | **CUT** | Not needed. The title of the act can carry the name. |
| § 3109.082 legislative policy | **CUT** | R.C. 3109.401 already states Ohio's policy that "the relationship between a child and each parent should be fostered." Rev. 2 makes a one-line amendment to it instead. |
| § 3109.083 definitions (six terms) | **CUT** | Only one definition was really needed, "substantially equal parenting time," and it now sits inside § 3109.044(I). "Parent," "parenting time," "abused child," and the others already have Ohio meanings. |

## B. Equal status and unmarried parents

| Rev. 1 | Fate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| § 3109.084(A)–(B) equal status; no preference by sex or marriage | **MERGED** | Now a one-sentence amendment to existing R.C. 3109.03, which already says parents "stand upon an equality" but covers only "husband and wife." |
| § 3109.084(C) codifying the *Troxel* fit-parent presumption | **CUT** | *Troxel* governs disputes between a parent and the state or a non-parent. Between two fit parents it decides nothing, and S.B. 174's coalition had already publicly resisted adding it. |
| § 3109.085(A)–(C) status when parentage is established; no separate action; status is not a schedule | **MERGED** | Now amended R.C. 3109.042(A)–(B), replacing the unmarried-mother rule in the same section where it lives today. |
| § 3109.085(D) **interim statutory minimum schedule applying by operation of law** | **CUT** | It conflicted with the core rule that status is not a schedule. Under Rev. 2, unmarried parents are in the same position married parents are in before any court order: both have equal status, and either may ask for a temporary order with a 28-day hearing. |
| § 3109.085(E) exceptions to the interim schedule | **CUT** | Fell away with (D). |
| § 3109.085(F) sex-neutral rape and sexual battery bar | **KEPT** | As a one-word change to existing R.C. 3109.042(B), now (D). |
| Repeal of R.C. 3109.042 | **REVERSED** | The section is now amended in place, not repealed. A reader looking for the unmarried-parent rule finds it where it has always been. |
| Amendment to R.C. 3111.13 | **CUT** | Amended 3109.042(A) already gives status when parentage is determined under Chapter 3111. Caught in the deletion pass. |

## C. The presumption

| Rev. 1 | Fate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| § 3109.086(A)–(B) two separate presumptions, for time and for decision-making | **MERGED** | One presumption, "shared parenting with substantially equal parenting time," plus § 3109.044(F) saying the two parts can be rebutted separately. |
| § 3109.086(B)(1)–(3) day-to-day and emergency decisions | **MERGED** | Into § 3109.044(G), in two sentences, with a new rule for deadlock: the existing arrangement continues until the court decides. |
| § 3109.086(C) list of proceedings covered | **CUT** | R.C. 3109.04(A) already lists them. § 3109.044 applies to every "first allocation." |
| § 3109.086(D) agreement carve-out; "no party requests" carve-out; protection-order carve-out | **KEPT / CUT** | Agreement and protection-order carve-outs kept. "No party requests equal time" cut: if nobody wants it, nobody will argue for it, and the parents can simply agree. |
| § 3109.086(E) ten enumerated rebuttal grounds | **CUT** | Ohio's 3109.04(F)(1) best-interest factors and 3109.04(C) already do this work. A second list invites fights over which list controls. |
| § 3109.086(F) four insufficient grounds | **KEPT** | Shortened to one sentence in § 3109.044(D), and "an allegation the court has not found true" added. |
| § 3109.086(G) Kentucky reverse presumption after two DV acts | **CUT** | Replaced by something simpler: the presumption **does not apply** to a parent under a protection order or with a 3109.04(C) conviction or adjudication. A second presumption pointing the other way is a second thing to litigate. |
| § 3109.086(H) savings clause | **MERGED** | Into § 3109.044(H). |
| § 3109.0861 written findings, four divisions | **MERGED** | One sentence: § 3109.044(E)(1). |
| § 3109.0862 domestic violence considered first; Kentucky anti-weaponization clause | **CUT / KEPT** | "Consider DV first" cut, because R.C. 3109.04(C) already requires it. The anti-weaponization rule (a protective parent's good-faith action is not counted as failing to facilitate) is kept in § 3109.046(F). |
| § 3109.0863 maximize-time fallback | **MERGED** | One sentence in § 3109.044(E)(2). |

## D. The statutory minimum floor

| Rev. 1 | Fate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| § 3109.087(A)–(B) Utah-style hour-by-hour minimum schedule (weekday evening, alternating weekends, holidays, four summer weeks, infant tier) | **CUT** | Its content made "every other weekend" the statutory norm, and the core goal is 50/50. Replaced by a one-sentence floor of 90 overnights a year (§ 3109.044(E)(3)), using the 90-overnight threshold Ohio lawyers already know from R.C. 3119.051. See Document 2, Part 5. |
| § 3109.087(C) departure grounds, (I) nine-item Track B grounds list | **MERGED** | One clause: less time only on agreement, the parent's own request, distance, or a finding that more time would endanger the child. Least restrictive means and written reasons kept. |
| § 3109.087(D) "the floor is not a target" | **KEPT** | One sentence, in bold. |
| § 3109.087(E) request that local rules conform | **CUT** | With no schedule left in the statute, there is nothing for local rules to conform to. Also removes a separation-of-powers target. |
| § 3109.087(F), (H) agreement carve-outs | **MERGED** | Into § 3109.044(C)(1) and (E)(3). |
| § 3109.087(G) graduated reintroduction | **MERGED** | Covered by the "more time would endanger the child's physical or emotional welfare" ground. A court can phase in time for a long-absent parent on that finding. |

## F. Interference, enforcement, child refusal

| Rev. 1 | Fate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| § 3109.088 civil "intentional interference with a parent-child relationship": definition, nine qualifying acts, numeric thresholds, speech savings clause | **CUT** | R.C. 3109.04(F)(1)(i) already makes "continuously and willfully denied the other parent's right to parenting time" a best-interest factor, and contempt already punishes it. The nine-act list was the bill's main First Amendment exposure. Removing it removes the need for the speech savings clause. |
| § 3109.0881 expedited enforcement: a separate motion with five required contents | **CUT** | Ohio already has a parenting-time contempt action (R.C. 2705.031(B)(2)). Rev. 2 adds a 28-day hearing and a simple prima facie rule to that existing action, in R.C. 3109.051(K)(2). |
| § 3109.0881(D) three-rung ladder (first, second, third finding) with five optional sanctions | **CUT** | R.C. 2705.05 already escalates contempt penalties for first, second, and third offenses (fines and jail). Rev. 2 keeps only mandatory make-up time and costs and fees on every finding, plus a change in circumstances on the third. |
| § 3109.0881(D)(3)(a) doubled make-up time after three findings (Texas) | **CUT** | Marginal value, and an argument every time about what "twice" means. Change in circumstances is the consequence that matters. |
| § 3109.0881(E) de minimis exception | **MERGED** | Into the single lawful-excuse list in 3109.051(K)(3). |
| R.C. 3109.051(K) "may" to "shall" make-up time | **KEPT** | Still the most important single change. |
| § 3109.0882 good-faith and false reports | **MERGED** | Into § 3109.046(F)–(G), which also adds the consequences of a knowingly false allegation. |
| § 3109.089 child refusal and safe harbor | **KEPT** | Shortened, and moved into 3109.051(K)(4). |
| § 3109.0891 relocation | **CUT** | R.C. 3109.051(G) already governs relocation notice, and distance is handled in § 3109.044(E)(3). |
| § 3109.0892 equal access to records | **CUT** | R.C. 3109.051(H)–(J), 3319.321, and 5104.039 already give access. A parent restored to residential parent and legal custodian has full access by that status. |
| Amendment to 3109.051(F)(2) on local-rule conformity | **CUT** | No schedule left to conform to. |
