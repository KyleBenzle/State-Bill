## 12. Provisions original to this draft

Listed in one place so the sponsor knows which parts have no enacted precedent anywhere in the
researched material, and are therefore the parts most likely to draw "untested" criticism.

1. **§ 3109.085(B)** — "No action, motion, complaint, or other separate proceeding is required."
2. **§ 3109.085(D)** — the statutory minimum as an interim schedule by operation of law on
   establishment of parentage.
3. **§ 3109.087(D)** — the express statement that the minimum is not the preferred schedule and
   that no presumption arises in its favour.
4. **§ 3109.0872(E)** — final hearing or explanation twelve months after a temporary order.
5. **§ 3109.0881(C)** — prima facie case from the order plus the missed period, shifting the
   burden of producing evidence.
6. **§ 3109.0881(E)** — the de minimis and agreed-change carve-out.
7. **§ 3109.088(C)** — no psychological syndrome required, and proof of one not sufficient.
8. **§ 3109.088(D)(2)** — the 50%-in-30-days percentage threshold.
9. **§ 3109.0882(A)**, final clause — a good-faith report may not be considered against the
   reporting parent.
10. **§ 3109.0882(E)** — lawful justification while a good-faith report is pending.
11. **§ 3109.089** — the entire section on a child's refusal and the duty to facilitate,
    including the no-physical-force limit.
12. **§ 2919.232(E)** — the codified non-exclusivity clause. *(The holding is *Parra*'s; putting it
    in a statute is original.)*
13. **Section 6** — the contingency mechanism.

Items 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 are, in this drafter's view, the five most valuable provisions in the bill
precisely because nobody else has them. Items 8 and 4 are the two most expendable if length or
opposition becomes a problem.
