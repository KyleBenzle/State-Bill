# DOCUMENT 10 — FINAL DECISIONS AND REMAINING TECHNICAL QUESTIONS (REVISION 5)

**No policy questions remain open.** All sponsor decisions are settled (list below). The items in
Part 2 are technical questions for outside counsel, LSC, fiscal staff, and implementing agencies. None
reopens policy.

## Part 1. Settled policy

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Equal status | Automatic on established paternity or parentage; status is not a schedule; no pre-order no-removal rule |
| Presumption | 50/50 (45%+) at first allocation; enumerated rebuttal grounds; 90-night floor |
| Child's preference | Age 12; followed unless endangering, impracticable, or not the child's own; written findings |
| Existing orders | Restoration without changed circumstances; first schedule motion at 50/50; no self-help |
| Court orders control | Only a court or a particular agreed deviation changes an order |
| Service costs | Voluntary; requesting parent may be charged; otherwise free to the non-agreeing parent, **including GALs** |
| Protection orders | Full-hearing orders switch off the presumption; ex parte and consent orders do not by themselves |
| Allegations | 90-day hearing; 36 months + one 45-day extension; clear and convincing for knowingly false |
| Enforcement | CSEA designated FSEA; optional; make-up time and a recorded violation; **no money payments**; referral thresholds |
| Support | Two components; **no percentage**; where-not-what accounting; prospective credit; no self-help |
| Linkage | Support and parenting enforcement stay separate |
| Criminal | 2919.23(A)–(D) unchanged; clarifications; 60 + 30 day prosecutor notice |
| Structure | No pilot; no companion bill; one-year delayed start for the FSEA and components |

## Part 2. Technical questions for review

**For outside family-law counsel**
1. **Superintendence rules vs. 3109.053.** Confirm that a statute allocating GAL and parenting
   coordinator costs controls over Sup.R. 8.02(A)(5)–(6) and local fee rules (art. IV, § 5(A)), and
   whether a companion request to the Supreme Court to amend Sup.R. 8.02 is advisable.
2. **"A parent who requests a service may be charged."** Confirm this reads correctly with
   3109.04(B)(2)(a), under which a GAL is mandatory on a parent's motion.
3. **Restoration motions in juvenile court.** Confirm that 3109.04(E)(1)(c) reaches custody orders
   entered under 2151.23(A)(2) through 3109.04's existing application there.

**For LSC (drafting form)**
4. Exact division lettering for the 3109.401(A)(5) finding and the 3109.04(E)(1)(c) insertion in the
   current text.
5. Whether LSC prefers separate conforming amendments to 3109.04(C), 3109.052, 3109.055, and 3109.12
   over the cross-reference and "notwithstanding" approach used here.
6. Full text of Sections 1 and 2 and the final title.
7. Conformity with S.B. 174 if it is enacted before introduction (Section 6).

**For ODJFS / child support program staff**

   302.56(g)).
10. Confirmation that 3125.10's non-IV-D cost-allocation sentence matches the agency's existing cost
    allocation plan.

**For LSC fiscal staff**

    comparison).
