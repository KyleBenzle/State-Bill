## D. Considered and rejected in Revision 3

| Idea | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Agency civil penalties or fees | A penalty for disobedience is contempt, which is judicial (*Hale*; *Zakany*). A fee invites the same challenge |
| Requiring agency findings to be admissible | Evid.R. 803(8) controls (art. IV, § 5(B)), and Ohio's rule excludes investigative findings |
| Compulsory agency conferences | Unnecessary. A non-response is treated as showing no excuse |
| Requiring the agency route before contempt | Would delay urgent cases. Left as Decision 3 for later |
| A 24-month window for the presumption on schedule motions (Option C) | Concentrates filings, then a cliff |
| Removing the 90-overnight floor | "Maximize" alone is unreviewable |
| A court-housed office (Michigan) | Keeps the workload in the courts |
