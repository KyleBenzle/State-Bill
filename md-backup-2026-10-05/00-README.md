# Ohio Equal Parenting and Parent-Child Relationship Protection Act
## Revision 5 — Final working draft (4 October 2026)

For review by outside Ohio family-law counsel and the Legislative Service Commission. Ohio and
federal law are stated as of 4 October 2026. Earlier revisions are in `archive-rev1/` to
`archive-rev4/`; primary sources are in `sources/`.

**The bill in four sentences:**
1. Two fit parents should start equal.
2. Money designated for a child should benefit the child.
3. A court order stays the order until the parents agree otherwise or a court changes it.
4. A court order must have a practical enforcement mechanism.

**Roles:** courts decide genuine disputes; the county child support agency, also designated the
Family Support Enforcement Agency (FSEA), handles routine enforcement; GALs investigate and
recommend; prosecutors decide charges; parents parent.

**Word and PDF copies** are in `for-lawmakers/`. Rebuild them after any edit with
`python3 make-handouts.py`. The bill builds in Ohio bill style (Courier, double-spaced, line
numbers, underline and strikethrough); the sponsor brief builds as a two-page handout.
