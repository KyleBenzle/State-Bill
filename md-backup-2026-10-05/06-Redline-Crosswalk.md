## Court orders control; services

| Current law | Change | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| Scattered case law; Sup.R. 8.06(A)(3); 6.42(B) | **R5:** one rule: the order stands until a court changes it or the parents agree to a particular deviation; no one else reduces, suspends, supervises, or conditions; GAL is evidence; parenting coordinator implements only | *Weisgarber*; *Gregory*; *In re Hoffman* |
| 3109.053: court "shall impose the cost" of classes and counseling; 3109.052: parents may be ordered to pay mediation; Sup.R. 8.02(A)(5): GAL compensation in the order | **R5:** parents may agree to pay; a requesting parent may be charged; otherwise a court-required service (**including GALs**) is free to the non-agreeing parent; nonpayment never delays or restricts | Sup.R. 8.02(I)(2)–(3) (extended); NEW |
